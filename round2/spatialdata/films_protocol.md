# Paired authored stereo and discrete-surround reference

Select 12 s from 60, 180, 300 and 420 s in Big Buck Bunny and Sintel. Selection is independent of acoustic content. Use the original authored six-channel FLAC and the corresponding separate original stereo FLAC. The four time windows per film are repeated measurements of one film. A six-speaker master specifies authored channels; it has no separate object coordinates. Excerpts are losslessly sliced from headers and seekpoint ranges in the Xiph master files, with each source byte range and SHA-256 retained.

Train one offline diagnostic downmix of Big Buck Bunny's 5.1 channels to the authored stereo. Fit four left/right-mirrored weights (front ipsilateral, centre shared, same-side surround, opposite-side surround) on that one film. Omit LFE, do not tune offsets, compare against the fixed baseline L=FL+0.707 FC+0.707 BL and R=FR+0.707 FC+0.707 BR. Evaluate waveform SI-SDR relative to the authored stereo on all four untouched Sintel windows. Stereo and 5.1 masters may be separately mastered; report same-time correlation and failures. No application change or claim about stereo-to-5.1 reconstruction or height channels follows.
