# Round-two spatial sources and external recording check

Only ingest official measured assets with verified reuse terms. Keep dataset-level licence, credit, piece-level provenance and SHA-256. Count paired physical microphones as a single signal and distinguish room responses from field recordings. Geographical recordings and reference renditions are never counted as independently measured source geometry. Archive members are addressed by name and verified against CRC.

The entire Nancy BinauRec subset is an external diagnostic split, with no fitting or model selection on any excerpt from that room. Select two distinct speech IDs per scenario (A–F) and nominal −5, 0, +5 dB folder. Choose the two lowest not yet selected IDs per condition when sorting first by scenario then by SNR. Select only measured with-head front left/right microphones. Download speech-shaped noise (SSN), paired speech presentation and actual physical speech+SSN mixture; no Freesound noise or music selected. Report speech quality as stereo waveform SI-SDR improvement over the real recorded mixture. Complete release RNNoise path should preserve dimensions/additivity. Distinct repeated presentations may be slightly desynchronized or have gain mismatch, so also report their closure error and a separate software-added matched-reference control. Do not label headphone recordings as phone microphone capture. Scope: external evaluation, not speaker identity tracking or FOA.
