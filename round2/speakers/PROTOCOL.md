# v0.2 speaker-path reassignment
Use both official Mini LibriSpeech archives (OpenSLR 31, CC BY 4.0; Vassil Panayotov and LibriSpeech contributors). These are mono recordings. Simulated paths/positions are documented separately. The shipped coefficients are trained from short segments of train-clean-5. We use dev-clean-2 exclusively for the independent evaluation and assert zero overlapping reader IDs. Hold out different original utterances even within one reader when comparing two examples. Use a fixed seed and retain the thresholds chosen before examining results.

Training fits nonnegative weights for a weighted absolute distance over pitch quantiles, periodicity and short-time cepstrum means and standard deviations. The objective ranks another excerpt from the same reader closer than a different reader. No per-reader template or speech is shipped in the model. Runtime spatially separates the two original microphone paths and compares both estimated speech tracks to enrolled reference voices over sliding time windows. Require reciprocal, persistent identity evidence before cross-fading the track assignments. Similar recordings, overlapping directions and other path changes still have no general identity solution.

Primary tests: balanced + mild reverberation, one stationary and one crossing scene per held-out listener pair and replicate, identities preserved at scoring, gate on worst speaker ordinary waveform SNR improvement >=3dB, also report harmful (<0dB), refusals, exact reconstruction and baseline comparison. Hold source position fixed before crossing so the oracle is known. Score on active overlap after a physical exchange completes. Use 3 different dry utterances per reader for enrollment, fixed/crossing shared test utterance and replicate. Separately rerun the original six-pair 54-case stress suite, without changing its cases or thresholds. Keep stationary returns, non-zero rejection, and crossing improvement separate; no claim of full moving-speech recovery. The previous public suite is a known regression set, not independent evaluation.
