# Materials and changes

- **EigenScape**, Marc Ciufo Green and Damian Murphy, University of York. Version DOI [10.5281/zenodo.2628463](https://zenodo.org/records/2628463), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Real 4th-order EigenMike field recordings and their first-order Lite editions, both ACN / SN3D. We excerpted the first eight seconds of each Lite recording, encoded as four-channel FLAC, and the first two seconds of eight matching fourth-order originals in 25-channel PCM. The papers and annotations retain their own provenance. The scene names describe environments, without isolated stems or individual-source directions.
- **Urban background sound recordings for virtual acoustics under various weather conditions at IHTApark**, Josep Llorca-Bofí, Jonas Heck, Christian Dreier and Michael Vorländer. Version DOI [10.5281/zenodo.11184517](https://zenodo.org/records/11184517), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). We excerpted four seconds from the unfiltered first-order and binaural files. The published paper (Sec. 2.2 and 3.1) identifies AmbiX B-format and binaural downmix by a generic decoder. The binaural and FOA files contain the same field performance. FLAC encoding may quantize 32-bit source data to 24-bit audio. [Article and methods](https://publications.rwth-aachen.de/record/996448/files/996448.pdf)
- **Audio recordings and soundscape assessments from the study “Indoor soundscape assessment: A principal components model of acoustic perception in residential buildings”**, Simone Torresin, Rossano Albatici, Francesco Aletta, Francesco Babich, Tin Oberman, Stefano Siboni and Jian Kang. Version DOI [10.5281/zenodo.10687363](https://zenodo.org/records/10687363), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). We excerpted eight seconds from seven edited AmbiX stimuli. The music/television captures remain reference-only; this project did not use them to train a model. The record's participant table is not in the corpus.

All files are excerpts. No original authors endorse Voice Hunter. The render experiment and derived scores are our own.
